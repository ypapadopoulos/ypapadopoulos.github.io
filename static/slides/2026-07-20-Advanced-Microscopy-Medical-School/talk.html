F-SHARP measures the \(E_{\mathrm{PSF}}\) inside the medium

Focus Scanning Holographic Aberration Probing
(F-SHARP)

  • Non-invasively measures the amplitude and phase of the electric field inside the inhomogeneous medium
  • This knowledge allows us to correct for aberrations and scattering
  • Does not rely on ballistic light
  • Correction is not limited by the memory effect (imaging still is)

Papadopoulos, I. N., Jouhanneau, J.-S., Poulet, J. F. A. & Judkewitz, B. Scattering compensation by focus scanning holographic aberration probing (F-SHARP). Nature Photonics.
